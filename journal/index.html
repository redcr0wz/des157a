Overlays Design Pattern Research

January 28, 2026

After reading Best Practices for Modals / Overlays / Dialog Windows by Naema Baskanderi, I have a better understanding on what defines an overlay (or modal) and the characteristics that makes up a well-designed overlay. Something that did stand out to me was that a good modal does not come up by surprise, but a lot of websites in my experience (typically stores) always use surprise overlays to promote their deals. Another part of the article I also found interesting was incorporating modals in mobile applications. It does state that you should not result in a modal in a mobile app and there are different ways of displaying that content. This article overall listed out essential features for a modal with good user experiences that will aid me in my website designs.

Research Form Design

January 17, 2026

After reading Best practices for form design by Salim Ansari, I feel like the overall general best practices to keep in mind when designing forms is to keep it simple, clear, and broken up. I do think the most important point I need to keep in mind is sizing the forms accordingly, since I do have the tendency to keep everything of equal size when it comes to designing forms. I also want to keep in mind that placeholder text should be outside of the text box because I have the preference of wanting my text boxes to look seamless. However, I do want to prioritize an effective user experience for my websites. I'll keep all of these practices in mind when working with my website designs.

In terms of a website form I've liked in the past, I enjoyed Google Fonts' form design because of how visually clear the dropdown titles and the selection boxes are.
